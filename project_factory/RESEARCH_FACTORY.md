# Research Factory

This is the generic evidence-first research layer used for hard prediction/mechanism problems.

It intentionally contains no private project-specific hypotheses or datasets.

## Flow

1. Define the causal hypothesis.
2. Identify the exact decision cutoff.
3. Separate variables observable at cutoff from variables randomized or revealed afterward.
4. Marginalize over unavailable future state instead of pretending it is known.
5. Freeze the hypothesis, outcome definition, evidence cutoff, holdout, metric, baseline, correction method, and stop rule.
6. Run independent roles: finder, independent researcher, falsifier, detectability engineer, blind evaluator, auditor.
7. Test prospectively.
8. Resolve only to VERIFIED, FAILED, or UNKNOWN.
9. Keep prediction-edge claims separate from economics/crowding claims.
10. Convert every discovered failure into a permanent regression rule.

## Promotion rule

No hypothesis is promoted because it is interesting or retrospectively impressive. It must survive the cutoff gate, leakage checks, a frozen chronological holdout, multiple-testing correction, prospective evidence, and replication.
