# Commercial Fork — Lead Rescue Voice

The hackathon project has a separate commercial path for field-service businesses.

## Current proof

A public, independent HVAC lead-routing proof-of-concept is already live at:

https://ava-hvac-lead-router.vercel.app

That site is **not** the Amazon MCP demo and is not presented as Alexa+ technology. It is evidence that the underlying customer problem can be packaged into a standalone business workflow.

## Commercial product direction

Target verticals:
- HVAC
- roofing
- electrical
- plumbing
- landscaping
- garage-door service
- junk removal

Core paid outcome:
- respond to valuable inquiries faster;
- keep urgent requests from getting buried;
- reduce forgotten follow-up;
- keep pricing and unusual commitments under owner control.

Possible packaging:
- fixed setup for a business's existing web form / CRM / inbox;
- recurring subscription for monitoring, lead ranking, follow-up preparation, and owner escalation.

## Rule

Competition claims and commercial claims remain separate. The live commercial proof does not count as evidence that the Alexa+/MCP implementation is deployed publicly; the Amazon branch CI and source remain the evidence for that technical claim.
